CRule IDs from v1.0.0

A citation of a rule in v1.0.0 can be followed here to its place in this version. A rule is changed when it survives under the same identifier with a different meaning, merged when another rule now states it, and retired when no rule in this version states it. An identifier of v1.0.0 that is neither in the table nor in the list of new rules after it was kept, with its meaning unchanged. Rules new to this version are listed after the table.

Every v1.0.0 rule identifier that was changed, merged or retired has a row here, with its fate and the reason; the others were kept.
